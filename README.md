# Chriz Darren Almonte - Undertale & 8-Bit Pixel Game Developer Portfolio

A fully responsive, retro pixelated game developer portfolio website inspired by **Undertale**, tailored for **Chriz Darren Almonte**, a **3rd Year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT)** student at **National College of Science and Technology (NCST)**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Collapsible Side Bar Navigation (Unobstructed Fullscreen View)**:
   - Replaced the fixed top bar with a sleek **Collapsible Side Bar (Drawer)** triggered by a floating **`[ ❤️ MENU ]`** button.
   - When closed, you get a 100% full-screen immersive view of your website without any header bar in the way.
   - When opened, the sidebar slides in from the left with logo, navigation links, sound toggle, Undertale Soul palette switcher, and CTA button.
   - Quick close via **`[ ✕ CLOSE ]`** button, clicking outside on the backdrop overlay, selecting any section link, or pressing the `Escape` key.

2. **Full Responsive Flexibility Across CP, Laptop & PC**:
   - **Cell Phones (CP: <= 768px)**:
     - Zero horizontal scrolling (`overflow-x: hidden`).
     - Fluid full-width stacking for all sections with comfortable touch targets (44px+).
     - Clamped typography so long pixel words and dialogue boxes never clip or overflow.
   - **Laptops & Tablets (768px - 1440px)**:
     - Adaptive multi-column grid layouts that fit cleanly without feeling cramped or stretched.
   - **PC & Ultrawide Monitors (1440px+)**:
     - Max-width centered containers (`max-width: 1180px`) with pixel borders and high readability.

3. **4 Featured Web Projects**:
   - 🎓 **1. BSLA Admissions & Student Portal**: Biringan Science & Leadership Academy Web Portal
   - 🎟️ **2. NCST Event Tracker Attendance for Incentives**: Campus Event Attendance & Student Incentive Points Logging System
   - 🍳 **3. Cooking Quest (Jerjerkings)**: Interactive Culinary Adventure Game
   - 💣 **4. JavaMinesweeper**: Multi-User Minesweeper & Highscore Leaderboards
